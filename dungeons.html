Forever Dungeon Loot

What every boss drops, with drop rates from recorded kills on Wowhead Forever. Only each boss's own loot is listed — no cloth, potions or random world drops. Tap an item for its full stats. Items marked STANDOUT are on the level lists. New Forever dungeons are marked New in Forever. Wowhead only has their quest rewards so far, not boss drops.

About the numbers

Drop rates are Wowhead's recorded drops divided by recorded kills, pulled on 21 Sep 2026. They're estimates. For rare spawns with fewer kills, expect them to shift a little.

Some bosses have no loot of their own. In the Stockade, only Bruegal Ironknuckle (rare) and Kam Deepfury drop named items; the rest only drop random world items. Lorgus Jett, Baron Aquanis and Roogug are the same. Jimmied Handcuffs appears in Bruegal's drop history but has no Forever tooltip, so it's left out.

Wowhead also lists Excavation Site: Wetlands, Manor Mistmantle and Half-Pint Tavern as dungeons, but has no quests, bosses or loot for them yet, so they are not shown.

Blackfathom Deeps and Gnomeregan also exist as Season of Discovery raids on Wowhead. These tables are the normal 5-player dungeons.
