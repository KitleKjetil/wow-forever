Forever Dungeon Loot

What every boss drops, with drop rates from recorded kills on Wowhead Forever, and every dungeon's quest rewards alongside them. Only each boss's own loot is listed — no cloth, potions or random world drops. Tap an item for its full stats. Items marked STANDOUT are on the level lists. New Forever dungeons are marked New in Forever, and Wowhead now has their boss drops as well as their quest rewards.

About the numbers

Drop rates are Wowhead's recorded drops divided by recorded kills, pulled on 25 Oct 2026. They're estimates. For rare spawns with fewer kills, expect them to shift a little.

Wowhead filled in the boss tables on 23 October, so most of this page is new. Where it records kills, the rate is shown; where it names the boss but has no kill history yet, the item says rate unknown. Baron Aquanis in Blackfathom Deeps is now the only boss here with no loot of its own.

Some items are listed for a dungeon without naming the enemy that drops them — usually trash rather than a boss. Those are grouped into a Drops in the dungeon card at the end of that dungeon rather than guessed onto a boss.

Where Wowhead does name the enemy and it turns out to be an ordinary trash mob rather than a boss, the card is marked Trash and is not counted as a boss. The test is the mob's level: a trash type covers a range, like a Defias Blackguard at level 19–20, while a boss or rare sits at a single level. Those drops are worth knowing about — they just are not something you can plan a run around.

Scarlet Monastery is one zone on Wowhead covering all four wings. Only the Graveyard and the Library are in range for this site, so the Armory and Cathedral bosses are left out, along with any SM drop Wowhead does not attribute to a named boss, since there is no way to tell which wing it belongs to.

Getting in. Most of these are open to either faction — Wailing Caverns, Shadowfang Keep, Blackfathom Deeps, Razorfen Kraul, Razorfen Downs and Scarlet Monastery all sit in neutral or reachable ground, whichever side you play. Two do not: The Stockade is inside Stormwind and Ragefire Chasm is inside Orgrimmar, so each is realistically one faction's only. The Deadmines and Gnomeregan have their entrances in Alliance territory, which makes them awkward rather than impossible for Horde.

The last three sit past this site's usual range. Razorfen Downs is 37–47, Uldaman and Maraudon are both 42–52, so their loot is for later rather than for the climb the level pages cover — those still stop at 30. They are here for completeness.

Wowhead also lists Manor Mistmantle and Half-Pint Tavern as dungeons, but still has no quests, bosses or loot for either, so they are not shown.

Blackfathom Deeps and Gnomeregan also exist as Season of Discovery raids on Wowhead. These tables are the normal 5-player dungeons.
