Reading the cards

+15 ilvl is the item level above its required level. Most gear before 10 sits at +3 to +5. The big outliers are quest rewards with no level requirement, and the new Hall of Thanes dungeon near Ironforge, whose quests only need level 9–10.

Engineering goggles need the listed Engineering skill but only level 5–10, which is why they sit at item level 43–58. They're mostly for twinks or alts with a high-level engineer.
