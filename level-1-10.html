Forever 1–10 Standouts

The strongest items you can realistically get between level 1 and 10. That's sorted by when you can actually earn each item (the quest's level, the dungeon boss's level, or when you can train the profession), not just its required level. Reading the cards

+8 ilvl on a card is the item level above the level you can realistically get it, not above its required level. Anything past +5 is doing something unusual.

The faction picker hides items whose source is locked to the other side. We take the quest's faction as the answer where we know it, because the item flag alone is not reliable — three world-drop BoEs on this site are flagged Horde-only, and a world drop reaches anyone through the auction house. Drops, world drops and crafted gear are treated as available to both.

This site was first built from the Alliance item list, so the Horde side is newer: Horde-only quest rewards were added on 26 Oct 2026, from the dungeons and from a scan of the Horde item list. If you find something missing on the Horde side, that is why. Battleground reputation gear is left out for both factions.

Most items that only require level 1–10 come from quests or dungeons meant for level 15 and up, so they're on the page for the level you can actually get them. This page is every uncommon or better reward from quests of level 13 and under, on both sides — the Alliance starting zones and class quests, and the Horde ones. Nothing rare is realistically available this early.

Both factions were gathered the same mechanical way from Wowhead's quest lists, so neither side's count is an artefact of how hard anyone looked. Until 27 Oct 2026 only the Alliance zones had been read, and a Horde reader saw 10 of 25 items where an Alliance one saw 25.

That gathering found 92 items, trimmed to these by two mechanical passes — no one's taste involved. Several class quests hand out the same weapon under different names, so those are merged into one row naming every quest you could get it from. An item is dropped only when something else here is available no later, usable by every class that could use it, no more faction- or race-locked, and at least as good on every stat with no effect lost. Where your race decides which version of a class quest you are offered, both versions stay.

Zephras Isle is a new Forever zone with a lot of level 9–12 quests, so many picks come from there — but every Zephras quest checked is locked to High Order Skyborne, a race sold as a Forever pack. Those items carry a note on the card.

The race lock is read from the quest's own Quick Facts on Wowhead, not guessed from the zone. The Skybreaker Bulwark is the one to watch: it reads like an ordinary level 10 class quest but is Skyborne-only, and Wowhead lists no class restriction on it at all.
