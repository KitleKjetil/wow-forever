Reading the cards

+8 ilvl on a card is the item level above the level you can realistically get it, not above its required level. Anything past +5 is doing something unusual.

Most items that only require level 1–10 come from quests or dungeons meant for level 15 and up, so they're on the page for the level you can actually get them. This page is every uncommon or better reward from Alliance quests of level 12 and under, minus the weak ones. Nothing rare is realistically available this early.

Zephras Isle is a new Forever zone with a lot of level 9–12 quests, so many picks come from there.
