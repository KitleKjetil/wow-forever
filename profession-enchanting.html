Enchanting Recipes

Enchants, and in Forever actual gear: staves, off-hand orbs and the three class relics. Those are the only relics you will see before the forties.

This page lists the 63 recipes you have to go and find — bought from a vendor, dropped by something, or given for a quest. The other 53 come from your trainer, so they need no guide. Everything here needs skill 225 or less, which is the ceiling a character can reach before level 35.

Reading this

A recipe's skill is what you need to learn it, not your character level. Profession skill is capped by tier: 75 as Apprentice, 150 as Journeyman, and 225 as Expert, which needs level 20. Nothing above 225 is reachable before 35, so this page stops there.

Vendor means a limited-supply or reputation vendor sells the recipe, Drop that something drops it, and Quest that a quest hands it over. Where Wowhead names the seller or the mob, it is shown; 54 of these 63 have a source type but no name yet, because the Forever database is still being filled in. The recipe name links to Wowhead, which will have the detail as it lands.

A PICK tag means the item it makes is one the rest of this site singles out as worth having. 12 further recipes at this skill range have no source recorded at all and are left off.
