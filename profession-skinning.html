Leather for leatherworking. No nodes and no recipes to chase: you skin what you kill, and the mob's level decides whether you can.

Skinning has no nodes to find and almost nothing to learn: you skin a corpse, and whether you can is decided by the mob's level rather than by any recipe. The whole profession is five entries, three of which are the skill ranks themselves.

Gathering skill is capped by tier rather than by your character level: 75 as Apprentice, 150 as Journeyman, and 225 as Expert, which needs level 20. Nothing past 225 is reachable before 35.
