Professions

Your trainer hands you most recipes without being asked. These pages cover the rest: the 743 recipes you have to go and find across all eight professions, what each one makes, and where it comes from — bought, dropped or given for a quest.

Everything listed needs skill 225 or less, the ceiling before level 35. What a profession actually makes for you is on the crafted sets page.

How to read these

Profession skill is capped by tier, not by your character level: 75 as Apprentice, 150 as Journeyman, 225 as Expert, which needs level 20. Nothing above 225 is reachable before 35, so these pages stop there.

Trainer recipes are counted but not listed — you get those by visiting the trainer, which needs no guide. What is worth a page is the rest: the vendor-only patterns, the ones that drop, and the ones a quest hands over.

Where Wowhead names the vendor or the mob, it is shown with its zone. A good share have a source type but no name yet, because the Forever database is still being filled in ahead of launch. Those rows say so rather than guessing.
