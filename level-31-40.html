What is on this page: dungeon boss drops, world drops, quest rewards from inside and outside a dungeon, the few pieces sold by ordinary vendors, and items Wowhead has no source for yet — those are listed at their required level and say so on the row, rather than being left out or given a guessed source. What is not: PvP rank and battleground reputation gear, left out on both sides at every level because it is earned in a battleground rather than while levelling, and crafted gear, which has its own page. What vendor gear costs is not in the data, so the price, and any reputation it may need, is worth checking in game.
