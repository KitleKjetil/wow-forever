Forever Crafted Sets

Every crafted piece in WoW Forever that a character under 31 can wear — 261 items across 28 sets. Forever replaced the old crafted greens with proper sets: nine pieces each, one flavor per playstyle, and no dungeon or drop luck involved. Each table lists the required level, the item level and the profession skill the recipe needs.

Reading the tables

Skill is the profession skill the recipe needs, not the required level. Most set pieces need far more skill than a character of that level will have, so treat the level column as "when it stops being wasted on you" and the skill column as "when you can actually make it".

The five mail and six leather sets share a slot pattern: belt at 12, chest and gloves at 15, boots at 17, legs and a hood at 20, a second chest at 22, and a helm and second legs at 25. Mail also gets three "Silvered" pieces mixed in. Nothing in these sets is Bind on Pickup, so a crafter can make them for you.

Class markers come from the same rules as the level pages: armor and weapon proficiency before 40, plus whether the piece carries a stat that class wants. Shamans are included because Forever lets the Alliance play them.
