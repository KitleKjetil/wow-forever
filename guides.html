How these are built

Each guide reads the same item data as the rest of this site — the level pages and the crafted sets together. Crafted gear is included because it is what actually fills the early armour slots: for most classes the drop and quest data leaves shoulders, wrists and hands empty well into the twenties.

Levels are the realistic ones used across the site: a quest reward sits two levels under the quest, a dungeon drop about three under the boss, and anything you can buy sits at its required level.

Classes with more than one job get a filter for it, so a paladin can read the page as a leveler, a tank or a healer. A few chains are gated by needing a group rather than by your level; those get a Worth planning for box instead of a misleading line in the chain.
