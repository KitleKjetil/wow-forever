Reading the cards

+8 ilvl on a card is the item level above the level you can realistically get it, not above its required level. Anything past +5 is doing something unusual.

Engineering gear only needs a low level, but the wearer needs the Engineering skill. Skill 76–150 needs level 10, 151–225 needs level 20 and 226–300 needs level 35.

Dungeon abbreviations: DM Deadmines, WC Wailing Caverns, SFK Shadowfang Keep, BFD Blackfathom Deeps. Items marked "source not on Wowhead yet" are new in Forever. They're placed at their required level.
