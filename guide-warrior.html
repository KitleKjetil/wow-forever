Leveling a Warrior

Warriors level on a big two-hander and tank dungeons with a one-hander and shield, so this page tracks both. Strength and Attack Power are the leveling stats; Defense, Block and Bonus Armor are the tanking ones. Mail is your armour the whole way to 40.

Each slot below is a chain of upgrades: the level you can realistically get the piece, what it is, and where it comes from. Anything that isn't actually better than what you already have is tucked into "other options" rather than cluttering the line.

The chains are complete to level 30. Past that they cover boss drops, world drops and quest rewards up to 50. What is still missing up there is vendor gear — which at those levels is mostly battleground reputation gear, left out on both sides — and crafted gear, which has its own page.

Worth planning for

Gear that a level alone won't get you, and that the slot chains above would otherwise bury.

The Whirlwind weapons from 30, with a group

Bath'rah the Windwatcher in Alterac Mountains runs a warrior-only chain — The Windwatcher, then Cyclonian, then The Summoning — that ends in a choice of Whirlwind Axe, Warhammer or Sword. All three are item level 40, about 35.5 damage per second, +16 Strength and +15 Stamina; pick whichever matches a weapon skill you already use.

That is roughly 18% more damage per second than the best two-hander you can otherwise hold at 30 — the single largest weapon jump available to a leveling warrior. Now that the dungeon chains run past 30 you can see how long it lasts: nothing beats it on damage until Ironaya's Ironshod Bludgeon in Uldaman at 37, and that one trades Strength for Stamina, so in practice the Whirlwind carries you to around 40.

The chain opens at level 30. What gates it is not your level but Cyclonian, a level 40 elite with 4,572 health, so bring a group. That is why it is not in the chains above: it would read as a solo pickup, which it is not.

How to read this

Weapon damage is everything. A two-hander two levels newer beats almost any armour upgrade, because your attacks and most of your abilities scale off the weapon's damage.

Warriors wear mail, so the crafted mail sets are the backbone of the armour slots. Nothing else fills shoulders, wrists or hands before the mid-twenties.

The faction picker hides pieces whose source is locked to the other side, taking the quest's faction as the answer where we know it. Drops, world drops and crafted gear reach both factions. This site was built from the Alliance item list, so Horde-only items are not covered yet — the Horde view is "what you can also get", not a complete list.

Levels are the realistic ones used across this site: a quest reward sits two levels under the quest, a dungeon drop about three under the boss, and anything you can buy sits at its required level. Crafted pieces are listed at their required level with the profession skill they need — most bind on pickup, so you need the profession yourself.

A chain only shows a piece when it beats the item level of everything before it. Sidegrades, and pieces that are better for the other job, are under "other options".

Past level 30 this guide covers the dungeons only. Everything shown from 31 up comes from Gnomeregan, Scarlet Monastery, Razorfen Downs, Uldaman or Maraudon — either off a boss or from one of those dungeons’ own quests. What is not there yet is world drops and quests outside a dungeon, because the item scans behind this site were built for levels 1–30. A thin chain up there means we have not catalogued the rest, not that nothing else exists.

Every dungeon’s own quest rewards are in, at all levels — 97 of them were missing until 25 Oct 2026, including the Stockade blues from The Fury Runs Deep. Horde-side dungeon quests are in too, and the faction picker hides them unless you switch sides.

The 21–30 page also lists by name a set of quest rewards worth planning for that land after 30. Those are outside the dungeons, so Wowhead records no source we can place them by, and they stay out of these chains.
