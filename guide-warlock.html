A warlock-only chain from Doan Karhan rewarding the Orb of Soran'ruk, item level 25 in the off-hand slot.

Wowhead lists it as a level 25 quest, required level 20, marked Dungeon — the shards come from inside an instance, so it is a group trip rather than a solo errand. Worth knowing because the off-hand slot is otherwise bare for a warlock until the mid-twenties.

How to read this

Stamina is a damage stat for a warlock. Life Tap turns health into mana, so a bigger pool is more casting, not just more survivability.

The Shadow cloth set is built for you; the other school sets are not, whatever their item level says.

Levels are the realistic ones used across this site: a quest reward sits two levels under the quest, a dungeon drop about three under the boss, and anything you can buy sits at its required level. Crafted pieces are listed at their required level with the profession skill they need — most bind on pickup, so you need the profession yourself.

A chain only shows a piece when it beats the item level of everything before it. Sidegrades, and pieces that are better for the other job, are under "other options".
