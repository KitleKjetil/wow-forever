Camping

BlacksmithingLeatherworkingTailoringEnchantingEngineeringAlchemyCookingFirst AidMiningHerbalismSkinningFishingCamping

Camping is new in Forever and is not quite a profession: it sits alongside the ones you have. You place a campsite, and every profession contributes three camp objects that give a buff to anyone who visits — yours or another player's.

Around level 5 a quest called The Great Outdoors asks you to /sit at the campfire by the quest giver. It sends you to train Cooking and craft a Basic Campfire from Simple Wood and Flint and Tinder, both sold by the Cooking trainer. A Basic Campfire holds three items, but you may only place one of them yourself - the other two come from other players visiting your camp.

The ones worth having while leveling

Fish Bowl is the standout: 8% increased stats for Fishing skill 20, which is an afternoon's work and costs you nothing since Fishing is secondary. Camp Chair gives 2% critical strike for Skinning 20, and Camp Tent gives rested experience worth up to 5% of a level for Leatherworking 20.

All three are tier 1, which means skill 20 and a quest from your trainer. There is no reason to be without the ones your professions cover.

Every camp object by profession

Tier 1 comes from a quest and your trainer at skill 20. Tier 2 needs skill 140 and the recipe has to be found — drop or vendor — which is what each profession page lists. Tier 3 is greyed out: it needs skill 300, and that needs Artisan at level 35.

How the tiers work

Tier 1 — skill 20, quest and trainer. Trivially reachable. Every profession hands you its tier 1 camp item at skill 20.

Tier 2 — skill 140, drop or vendor. Reachable by level 20, when Expert lifts your cap to 225. The recipe has to be found - which is what the profession pages on this site list.

Tier 3 — skill 300, not recorded yet. Needs Artisan, which requires level 35, so it is out of reach for anything this site covers.

Cooking is the one profession that breaks the pattern: its campfires sit at skill 20, 90 and 200 rather than 20, 140 and 300, and the campfire itself is what sets how many objects your camp can hold — three at Basic, five at Journeyman, ten at Expert.

Tier 2 recipes are the interesting ones, because you have to go and find them. Each is listed on its profession's page with the source Wowhead records for it.
