The Tempest's Weapons from 30

A five-quest Alliance shaman chain — Call of Air, Elemental Aid, Storm Earth and Fire, Heed My Call, then The Tempest's Weapons — starting with Veyric Thunderhame and finishing at Bath'rah the Windwatcher in Alterac Mountains.

It rewards a choice of Lightning's Grasp (+46 spell power), Tidebringer's Claw (+86 healing) or Rage of the Storm (a 35.6 damage-per-second two-hander that boosts Stormstrike), and hands you Elementalist's Guard as well — a shield you keep whichever weapon you choose. All are item level 40.

Wowhead lists it as a level 40 quest with a required level of 30. Bath'rah runs the warrior Whirlwind chain from the same spot, so the two tend to get done on one trip.

How to read this

Weapon damage matters for enhancement because your attacks scale off it, but a shaman also wants Intellect for the shocks and heals that keep you moving. Pieces carrying both are worth more than their item level.

The Totemic crafted leather set is built for that overlap: Strength with spell power on every piece.

Levels are the realistic ones used across this site: a quest reward sits two levels under the quest, a dungeon drop about three under the boss, and anything you can buy sits at its required level. Crafted pieces are listed at their required level with the profession skill they need — most bind on pickup, so you need the profession yourself.

A chain only shows a piece when it beats the item level of everything before it. Sidegrades, and pieces that are better for the other job, are under "other options".
