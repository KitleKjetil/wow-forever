Reading the cards

+10 ilvl is the item level above its required level. Most rares in this bracket sit at +5. The big outliers are quest rewards from the classic Stranglethorn, Desolace and Gnomeregan chains, which have much higher item levels than their requirement.

Dungeon abbreviations: SFK Shadowfang Keep, BFD Blackfathom Deeps, Stocks Stormwind Stockade, Gnomer Gnomeregan, RFK Razorfen Kraul, SM Scarlet Monastery. Items marked source not listed are Forever-new items Wowhead hasn't tagged with a drop source yet.

The filter also returns Darkspear Raiders gear, but that's a Horde faction, so it's left out here. The Theramore Expeditionary Force sells Alliance copies of the same items (seals, Rebels' Rugged Reaper, Theramore Signet).
