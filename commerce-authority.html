Azeroth Commerce Authority

BlacksmithingLeatherworkingTailoringEnchantingEngineeringAlchemyCookingFirst AidMiningHerbalismSkinningFishingCampingCommerce Authority

An Alliance faction that sells profession recipes for Merchant's Favor rather than gold. Loot crates from around the world, fill them, and hand them in at the camp for Merchant's Favor. You then spend Merchant's Favor on recipes, mounts, pets and cosmetics. Craftsman's Writs are a second source and may be available from level 10. The Horde equivalent is Durotar Supply and Logistics.

It matters more than a normal vendor because it is the single biggest source of recipes you cannot learn from a trainer: 237 of them at skill 225 or less, including whole crafted sets.

237 recipes in range 7 professions paid in Merchant's Favor

Where to go

Alliance: Three Corners, Redridge Mountains — between Elwynn Forest and Lakeshire, just outside town. /way #44 10.5 72.7

Horde: The Barrens, just outside Crossroads. /way #17 42 34

The recipe seller on the Alliance side is Alynsia /way #44 11.2 71.6

Why this page exists

The profession pages on this site list the recipes a trainer will not give you, and a large share of them came back from Wowhead as "Vendor" with no seller named. This faction is the answer for 237 of them, so those rows now name it and show the price.

Forever is still in beta and Wowhead's guide says the faction's details may change before launch — Craftsman's Writs in particular are only partly documented, beyond being available from around level 10.
