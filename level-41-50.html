Levels 41–50

Boss drops, world drops and quest rewards you can realistically get between 41 and 50, sorted by the level you can actually get it — a dungeon drop three levels under the boss, a quest reward two under the quest, a world drop at the level you can wear it.

125 items18 slotspulled 27 Oct 2026
What is on this page: dungeon boss drops, world drops, quest rewards from inside and outside a dungeon, and the handful of pieces sold by ordinary vendors. What is not: PvP rank and battleground reputation gear — 321 of the 330 vendor items in this range are the Knight's, Blood Guard's, Sergeant Major's and Alterac Valley sets, left out on both sides at every level because they are earned in a battleground rather than while levelling — plus crafted gear, which has its own page, and items Wowhead records no source for. What vendor gear costs is not in the data, so the price, and any reputation it may need, is worth checking in game.
