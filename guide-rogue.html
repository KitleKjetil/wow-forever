Leveling a Rogue

Rogues want Agility, weapon speeds that suit the build, and as little time spent eating as possible. There is no self-heal anywhere in the kit before 30, so Stamina is worth more than it looks. Leather until 40.

Each slot below is a chain of upgrades: the level you can realistically get the piece, what it is, and where it comes from. Anything that isn't actually better than what you already have is tucked into "other options" rather than cluttering the line.

The chains are complete to level 30. Past that they cover the dungeons only — boss drops and the dungeons’ own quest rewards, up to Maraudon at 50. World drops and quests outside a dungeon are not catalogued up there yet, so read a short chain past 30 as a gap in the data rather than a gap in the game.

How to read this

Each hand wants a different weapon. A slow main hand suits Ambush and Backstab openers; a fast off hand suits everything else. The chains below rank on damage per second, so check the speed before you equip.

With no heal in the kit, the way to cut downtime is more Stamina and shorter fights — both argue for keeping weapons current over chasing armour.

The faction picker hides pieces whose source is locked to the other side, taking the quest's faction as the answer where we know it. Drops, world drops and crafted gear reach both factions. This site was built from the Alliance item list, so Horde-only items are not covered yet — the Horde view is "what you can also get", not a complete list.

Levels are the realistic ones used across this site: a quest reward sits two levels under the quest, a dungeon drop about three under the boss, and anything you can buy sits at its required level. Crafted pieces are listed at their required level with the profession skill they need — most bind on pickup, so you need the profession yourself.

A chain only shows a piece when it beats the item level of everything before it. Sidegrades, and pieces that are better for the other job, are under "other options".

Past level 30 this guide covers the dungeons only. Everything shown from 31 up comes from Gnomeregan, Scarlet Monastery, Razorfen Downs, Uldaman or Maraudon — either off a boss or from one of those dungeons’ own quests. What is not there yet is world drops and quests outside a dungeon, because the item scans behind this site were built for levels 1–30. A thin chain up there means we have not catalogued the rest, not that nothing else exists. Below 30 the chains are complete.

The 21–30 page also lists by name a set of quest rewards worth planning for that land after 30. Those are outside the dungeons, so Wowhead records no source we can place them by, and they stay out of these chains.
